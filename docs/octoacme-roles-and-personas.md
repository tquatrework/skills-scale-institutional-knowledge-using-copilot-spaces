# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Business Analysts

### Role Summary
Business Analysts refine business needs into clear, testable requirements and process changes. They help teams make shared decisions based on scope, constraints, and stakeholder intent.

### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document functional and non-functional requirements
- Clarify business rules, workflows, and edge cases
- Maintain requirement traceability from request to delivery
- Support acceptance criteria definition and change impact analysis

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** Provide requirement clarification, examples, and edge-case detail during implementation.
- **Product Managers:** Partner on problem framing, requirement quality, and prioritization trade-offs.
- **Project Managers:** Help sequence requirement dependencies and keep scope changes visible in plans.

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide technical direction, feasibility, and system-level design to ensure delivery quality and long-term maintainability.

### Responsibilities
- Define architecture boundaries, integration patterns, and technical standards
- Review solution designs for feasibility, scalability, and risk
- Identify technical dependencies and implementation constraints early
- Mentor developers and support technical decision records

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** Guide design decisions, unblock implementation risks, and review technical approaches.
- **Product Managers:** Translate product goals into feasible technical options and trade-offs.
- **Project Managers:** Surface technical risks and dependency impacts that affect schedule and sequencing.

---

## QA Lead / Test Coordinator

### Role Summary
QA Leads coordinate quality strategy across teams to ensure features are verifiable, test coverage is appropriate, and release quality is measurable.

### Responsibilities
- Define test strategy across functional, integration, and regression scopes
- Coordinate test planning, environment readiness, and execution timelines
- Track defect trends and ensure critical issues are resolved before release
- Confirm acceptance readiness against documented criteria

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** Align on testability, defect triage, and coverage expectations.
- **Product Managers:** Validate acceptance criteria and customer-facing behavior quality.
- **Project Managers:** Communicate quality risks, test milestones, and release-go/no-go inputs.

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers own release readiness coordination, deployment planning, and post-release verification to reduce delivery risk.

### Responsibilities
- Define release checklists, timelines, and rollout sequencing
- Coordinate approvals, change windows, and communication plans
- Ensure rollback, monitoring, and validation steps are prepared
- Confirm release evidence and handoff completion

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** Coordinate cut-off timing, deployment steps, and production verification activities.
- **Product Managers:** Align release timing with customer commitments and feature readiness.
- **Project Managers:** Integrate release gates and cross-team dependencies into project plans.

---

## Support / Operations Representatives

### Role Summary
Support and Operations representatives provide production context, operational readiness, and incident insights that improve delivery reliability.

### Responsibilities
- Share recurring support themes and operational constraints during planning
- Prepare runbooks, monitoring expectations, and escalation paths
- Monitor release impact and coordinate incident response handoffs
- Feed production learnings back into backlog and process improvements

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** Share production issues, logs, and recovery feedback to improve resiliency.
- **Product Managers:** Inform prioritization using customer-impact and support-volume signals.
- **Project Managers:** Coordinate incident follow-up actions and operational dependencies.

---

## Security / Compliance Reviewers

### Role Summary
Security and Compliance reviewers assess whether planned and delivered changes meet security controls, policy expectations, and regulatory obligations.

### Responsibilities
- Review requirements and designs for security and compliance impact
- Identify control requirements, audit evidence needs, and policy gaps
- Validate threat mitigations and compliance sign-off criteria
- Support incident reviews for security and compliance learnings

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **Developers:** Advise on secure implementation patterns and required control evidence.
- **Product Managers:** Clarify policy constraints that influence scope and feature behavior.
- **Project Managers:** Ensure security/compliance checkpoints are included in timelines and risk tracking.
